THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROCESS

I’m Emmanuel, a product designer who enjoys figuring out why things work the way they do, why they sometimes don’t, and how they could work better.

My process often starts with questions. What’s confusing people? What’s stopping them from completing a task? Are we solving the right problem in the first place?

I enjoy getting into the research, exploring possibilities, challenging assumptions and translating what I learn into experiences that make sense to people.

I care about the visual craft just as much. I enjoy shaping interfaces, building design systems, exploring interactions and giving products a visual identity that feels considered.

And because I like seeing ideas become real, I occasionally take things further by building them myself.
